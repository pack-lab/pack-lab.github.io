Tairan Liu will serve as the Session Chair for the Multi-Agent and Networked Systems II session at the 2026 6th Modeling, Estimation and Control Conference (MECC 2026). Sponsored by the American Automatic Control Council (A2C2) in cooperation with IFAC, MECC 2026 is taking place from October 25–28, 2026, at the Tempe Mission Palms in the Phoenix metropolitan area, Arizona.

The session highlights recent theoretical and applied advances in distributed control, coordination, and estimation across networked and multi-agent systems. If you are attending MECC 2026 this week, we invite you to join the session and connect with our group!
